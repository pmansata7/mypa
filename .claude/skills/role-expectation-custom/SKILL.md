# Role Expectation Framework

A structured template for documenting role-level expectations across problem solving, execution, communication, leadership, and impact. Use this skill to create role expectations, self-assess against them, or prepare for calibration conversations.

## Use Cases

- **Create role expectations**: Define what "good" looks like for a specific role/level
- **Self-assessment**: Evaluate performance against documented expectations
- **Calibration prep**: Prepare evidence and examples for performance discussions
- **Career planning**: Understand gaps between current and target level

## Process

### Step 1: Select Mode

Ask the user:

```
How would you like to use this framework?

1. **Create New** - Document expectations for a role/level
2. **Self-Assess** - Evaluate yourself against existing expectations
3. **Calibration Prep** - Gather evidence and examples for review
4. **Gap Analysis** - Compare current vs. target level expectations
```

---

### Step 2A: Create New Role Expectations

Gather information through conversation:

1. **Role basics**
   - What is the skill/competency name? (e.g., "Strategic Execution", "Technical Leadership")
   - What level is this for? (e.g., Senior, Lead, Principal)
   - Brief description of scope, ambiguity, ownership, and impact expected

2. **Walk through each dimension** - For each, ask what behaviors/outcomes are expected:
   - Problem Solving & Strategic Thinking
   - Execution & Process Ownership
   - Executive Communication & Influence
   - Cross-Functional Leadership & Collaboration
   - Scope & Impact

3. **Define performance indicators**
   - What does "exceeds expectations" look like?
   - What does "meets expectations" look like?
   - What signals "developing / not yet meeting"?

4. **Generate the document** using the template below

---

### Step 2B: Self-Assessment Mode

1. Ask which role expectation document to assess against (or create one first)
2. For each dimension, ask:
   - "How would you rate yourself? (Exceeds / Meets / Developing)"
   - "What specific evidence supports this rating?"
3. Identify strongest areas and development priorities
4. Capture specific examples for each dimension

---

### Step 2C: Calibration Prep Mode

1. Ask which role/level and review period
2. For each dimension, gather:
   - 2-3 specific examples with measurable outcomes
   - Key stakeholders who can validate
   - Any gaps or areas of growth
3. Draft manager notes section with:
   - Strengths to highlight
   - Growth areas to acknowledge
   - Overall performance recommendation

---

### Step 2D: Gap Analysis Mode

1. Ask for current level and target level
2. Compare expectations across each dimension
3. Identify specific gaps between levels
4. Create development plan to close gaps

## Template Reference

Below is the full template for quick copy/paste:

```markdown
# Skill: {{Skill Name}}

## Skill Summary
> Brief description of what this skill represents at Adobe, focusing on scope, ambiguity, ownership, and impact.
>
> Example: Ability to independently define ambiguous problem spaces, drive high-impact initiatives end-to-end, and influence outcomes through rigorous thinking and effective execution.

---

## Level Expectations

### {{Level Name (e.g., Senior, Lead, Principal)}}

#### Problem Solving & Strategic Thinking
- {{Defines ambiguous problem spaces and frames the right questions.}}
- {{Develops clear, compelling, and testable hypotheses grounded in business context.}}
- {{Evaluates trade-offs, risks, and downstream implications when making strategic decisions.}}
- {{Prioritizes initiatives based on impact, feasibility, and strategic alignment.}}
- {{Secures appropriate leadership alignment before committing significant resources.}}

#### Execution & Process Ownership
- {{Independently owns a portfolio of initiatives rather than individual tasks.}}
- {{Applies a structured, repeatable approach to driving work forward.}}
- {{Effectively distinguishes between analytics needs, solution gaps, escalation, and ideation.}}
- {{Drives initiatives end-to-end from problem definition through launch and iteration.}}
- {{Seeks input and thought partnership appropriately while maintaining accountability.}}

#### Executive Communication & Influence
- {{Communicates clearly and concisely with senior and executive stakeholders.}}
- {{Grounds recommendations in rigorous thinking, hypotheses, and evidence.}}
- {{Clearly articulates trade-offs and decision rationale.}}
- {{Builds executive confidence through consistent focus on high-impact work.}}

#### Cross-Functional Leadership & Collaboration
- {{Leads through influence across cross-functional partners.}}
- {{Prioritizes business outcomes over consensus or relationship maintenance.}}
- {{Asks difficult or uncomfortable questions to challenge assumptions.}}
- {{Builds long-term credibility by elevating thinking and results across teams.}}

#### Scope & Impact
- {{Impact extends beyond individual projects to shaping direction across a problem space or portfolio.}}
- {{Operates effectively in high-ambiguity environments with multiple stakeholders.}}
- {{Work produces measurable, repeatable, and scalable business impact.}}

---

## Performance Indicators

### Exceeds Expectations
- {{Consistently operates beyond defined scope and influences broader strategy.}}

### Meets Expectations
- {{Consistently delivers at the expected level of ownership, rigor, and impact.}}

### Developing / Not Yet Meeting
- {{Requires additional support to handle ambiguity, scope, or influence effectively.}}

---

## Evidence & Examples
- {{Project / initiative name}}
- {{Business problem addressed}}
- {{Hypothesis and approach}}
- {{Outcome and measurable impact}}
- {{Key stakeholders and influence demonstrated}}

---

## Manager Notes
- {{Calibration notes, gaps, strengths, and growth areas}}
```
