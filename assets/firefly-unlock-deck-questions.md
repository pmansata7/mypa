## From comments (questions and requests)

- **Plans/pricing slide:**  
  Can we add somewhere breakdown of 3 different plans (adjust for the fact that $200 just recently launched) – to get a sense of what people are preferring?

- **Usage slide:**  
  I wonder if we also add a metric of % of people who haven't used any credits (since we know it's a decent amount)?

- **Usage segment slide:**  
  Can we add a split in % for casual/core/dormant/power? Also can we put dormant first (so rows go in ascending order of usage)?  
  *(And: key message = customers purchase FF regardless of usage segment.)*

- **Firefly Launch vs Express slide:**  
  Can we have a longer trend and projections of what that potentially means for Firefly with extended trends?

- **Retention table slide:**  
  Is it M2M only or everyone? What's the M2M / PUF ratio – is it different for Pro vs Standard? (Or for add-on vs initial purchase – may be worth adding to one of the prior slides.)

- **First 30 days / churn slide:**  
  Based on engagement, can we extend usage to 3 months and show multiple curves: people who cancelled after 1 month (usage in month 1), after 2 months, after 3 months, and people who haven’t cancelled after 3 months?

- **Insights to Action slide:**  
  Can we also add a slide with insights and actions at the end, e.g. “dormant people use FF → consider it as a resurrection tool (e.g. extra free credits)”?

- **Add-on retention slide:**  
  Can we add qualifier on how much better (e.g. x times better at month y)?

- **Retention slide (title/subheader):**  
  The title and subheader are repetitive; can we add a secondary insight of how much better retention is and whether it’s consistent across different SKUs?  
  Also, can you highlight key figures in the table to focus on?

- **Challenges in tracking slide:**  
  Add something about challenges on the customer side: navigating what does and does not generate credits, and how many credits they have left (especially when used in-product).
