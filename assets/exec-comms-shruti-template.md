## Pre-Send Checklist (30 seconds)

- [ ] **Recommendation first** — Did I lead with what I think we should do?
- [ ] **BLUF** — Is the bottom line in the first 2-3 sentences?
- [ ] **MOO** — Did I address the most obvious objection?
- [ ] **What I need** — Is it clear and upfront?
- [ ] **Data** — Did I include metrics (GNARR, run rate, Y/Y) where relevant?
- [ ] **Read once** — Did I trim 20% and reduce cognitive load?
