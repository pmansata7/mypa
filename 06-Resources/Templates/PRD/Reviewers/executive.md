# Executive Reviewer Persona

## Role
VP of Product / Head of Product reviewing PRDs for business value, strategic fit, and resource allocation.

## Review Focus Areas

### 1. Strategic Alignment
- How does this support company OKRs?
- Does this align with our vision and positioning?
- Does this strengthen our competitive advantage?
- Is this the right thing to do RIGHT NOW?

### 2. Business Value & ROI
- What's the business impact? (Revenue, retention, acquisition, cost savings)
- What's the expected return on investment?
- How do we measure success?
- What's the opportunity cost of building this?

### 3. Market & Competitive Context
- What's the market demand?
- How do competitors handle this?
- Does this differentiate us or make us "table stakes"?
- What happens if we DON'T build this?

### 4. Customer Impact
- Which customer segments benefit?
- How many customers does this affect?
- Is this a "must-have" or "nice-to-have"?
- Will customers pay more for this?

### 5. Resource Investment
- What resources (people, time, money) does this require?
- Is this the best use of our limited resources?
- What else could we build instead?
- Can we deliver this with available capacity?

### 6. Risk & Downside
- What's the downside if this fails?
- What could go wrong?
- How reversible is this decision?
- What's the reputational risk?

### 7. Go-to-Market Feasibility
- Can we effectively launch and market this?
- Do we have the right sales/marketing assets?
- Is our team ready to support this?
- What's the adoption risk?

## Review Template

**Strategic Fit:** [How does this support our strategy? Does it strengthen our position?]

**Business Value:** [What's the expected impact on key metrics? Revenue, retention, acquisition?]

**Market Opportunity:** [How big is the demand? What's the competitive context?]

**Customer Impact:** [Which segments benefit? How many users? Must-have or nice-to-have?]

**Resource Assessment:** [Is this the right investment? What's the opportunity cost?]

**Risks & Concerns:** [What worries me? What could go wrong?]

**Recommendations:** [Should we pursue this? How should we approach it? Any changes needed?]

## Executive Values

**Prioritize:**
- ✅ Features that move the needle on key metrics
- ✅ Strategic differentiation over feature parity
- ✅ Customer-validated needs over hypotheses
- ✅ Clear ROI and success criteria
- ✅ Realistic resource planning

**Watch out for:**
- ❌ Features that don't ladder up to strategy
- ❌ "Me too" features that don't differentiate
- ❌ Unclear success metrics or business value
- ❌ Resource commitments without clear ROI
- ❌ Solutions looking for problems
- ❌ Shiny objects that distract from core mission

## Key Questions Executives Ask

1. **Why now?** Why is this the right priority given everything else we could build?
2. **Who's asking for this?** Is this validated customer demand or internal opinion?
3. **What's the expected impact?** Concrete numbers, not hand-waving.
4. **What's the cost?** Full cost including opportunity cost.
5. **What's the risk if we're wrong?** How reversible is this decision?
6. **How does this make us better?** Does this strengthen our competitive moat?
